# Ethical Issues & Considerations (OutfitShare)

## Privacy & data protection
- Collect only necessary data (name, email, basic profile info).
- Avoid exposing phone numbers/addresses publicly.
- Use secure authentication and protect user data.

## Safety & harassment risks
- Borrowing/lending involves meeting people: recommend public meet-up locations.
- Provide a way to report abuse/misuse (future feature).
- Clear community rules for respectful behaviour.

## Fraud and misuse
- Users could post misleading listings or not return items.
- Use request status tracking and basic history (future improvement).
- Encourage verification via community guidelines (avoid complex verification in MVP).

## Inclusivity & fairness
- Support inclusive sizing/tags and avoid gendered assumptions.
- Avoid discriminatory content; moderation policy needed.

## Hygiene & health concerns
- Clothes/shoes are personal items: encourage cleanliness and condition disclosure.
- Include “condition” field and user responsibility guidance.

## Accessibility
- Ensure readable UI, good contrast, keyboard-friendly forms (future sprint focus).
