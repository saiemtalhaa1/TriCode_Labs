# User Personas – OutfitShare

## Persona 1: Aisha Khan (Borrower)
- Age: 20
- Role: University student
- Goal: Borrow a formal outfit for an interview without spending a lot.
- Needs: Easy search by size/category, clear photos, safe meet‑up.
- Frustrations: Expensive clothes for one‑time use, safety concerns.
- Scenario: Aisha searches for a blazer, sends a 7‑day request, meets the lender on campus, and returns the item on time.

## Persona 2: Daniel Lewis (Lender)
- Age: 28
- Role: Local community member / alumni
- Goal: Lend unused outfits and help others.
- Needs: Clear requests, accept/decline control, availability dates.
- Frustrations: Late returns, unclear communication.
- Scenario: Daniel lists a suit, accepts a request, meets the borrower publicly, and marks the item as returned.
