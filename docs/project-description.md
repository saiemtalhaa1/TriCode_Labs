## Project Scope and Policies

### Target Users
**Students and local community members** who want to lend or borrow outfits for interviews, formal events, and special occasions.

---

### Borrow Duration Policy
- **Standard lending period:** 7 days  
- **Maximum extension:** Up to 14 days (requires lender approval)

---

### Fair Use Policy
- Extensions beyond 7 days require **explicit approval from the lender**
- **No payments or late fees in the MVP version**
- Optional **late fee or donation feature** may be considered in future versions

---

### Supported Categories
Users can share and borrow the following items:

- **Suits and blazers**
- **Dresses**
- **Shoes**
- **Accessories** (ties, belts, bags)
- **Shared essentials:**
  - Jackets and coats
  - Formal shirts
  - Event-appropriate accessories

---

### MVP Constraints (to Keep the Project Achievable)
To ensure the project remains achievable within the coursework timeline, the following features are **excluded from the MVP:**

- ❌ No payments or financial transactions  
- ❌ No delivery or shipping functionality  
- ❌ No complex identity verification system  

---

### Safety Guidelines (High Level)
To promote safe and responsible usage:

- Handovers should occur in **safe public locations** (e.g., university campus)
- Users should **avoid sharing personal addresses or private contact details publicly**
- Safety, moderation, and reporting features may be enhanced in future sprints
