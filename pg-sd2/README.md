Project Name: OutfitShare (TriCode Labs)
Concept: A community-based platform where students and local residents can lend and borrow outfits for job interviews and special occasions, reducing cost and waste while building community connections.

Problem: Many people need formal outfits for interviews/events but do not want to buy expensive clothing for one-time use. At the same time, many community members own outfits that are rarely used.

Target Users: University students and local community members.

MVP Features (Sprint scope):

Create an account and profile

Create an outfit listing (category, size, condition, description, photos, availability)

Browse and filter listings (by category, size, gender-neutral sizing tags)

Request to borrow an item for up to 7 days

Lender can accept/decline requests

Status tracking (pending, accepted, declined, returned)

“My Listings” and “My Requests” dashboard

Borrow Duration Policy:

Standard lending period: 7 days

Optional extension: up to 14 days, only with lender approval

No payments in MVP (community-sharing focus); any fee/donation feature is a potential future enhancement.

Out of Scope for MVP (to keep it achievable):

Payments and deposits

Delivery / shipping logistics

Complex identity verification

Rating system (optional future feature)

Safety & Community Guidelines (high level):

Users should meet in safe, public locations (e.g., campus)

Do not publicly share personal phone numbers/addresses

Report inappropriate behaviour (future feature)
